Each specimen selects the family named in its heading and Follow Device Settings; its current device mode is Light or Dark as shown. The paired miniature previews are decorative depictions of each family’s Light and Dark surfaces/actions.

## Palette and state boards

The full palette hex values and semantic purposes appear in [tokens](TOKENS.md). The status board repeats five distinct states in all six appearances: Preview · Not saved; Saving preferences; Preferences saved; Preference changed elsewhere; Couldn’t save preferences. Supporting copy distinguishes local preview, acknowledgement, conflict review and retry. They are separate examples, not concurrent messages.
