# M4-R military cross-project — Six U.S. service physical-readiness standards (16 October 2026)

**Scope:** Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard; service-wide in-service testing plus identified occupational/combat variants. This is a **publicly sourced research reference, not a compliance certificate or authoritative score calculator**. Versions and exceptions matter. "Military branch" below denotes armed service, **not** Git branches. Owner's separate **Super Soldier LMS** remains a distinct civilian educational product.

**Evidence-method limitations:** Official service publications/fitness portals take precedence over press stories and commercial calculators. Versioned policy and component descriptions are better verified than **every** age/sex, altitude, occupation, special-pathway and exemption score-chart row. Full raw-score-to-point matrices **were not transcribed or validated**; chart-dependent precise individual pass projections must remain unavailable until source/edition, normalization and test-retest qualification. Policies can change without a release.

**Source precedence:** official current policy/order + issued score chart, then test administration and exceptions manual, then official service explanatory page, then scientific research on preparation, and only then outside commentary. A recruiting initial-entry minimum is not an annual in-service standard. A combat occupational qualification is not the baseline service test. An old number from a superseded edition is not current truth.

## How “pass and exceed” must be computed (original model proposal)

- **Input tuple:** `service`, `test_family`, `component_status`, `occupation_or_mos`, `service_component`, `accession_vs_in_service`, `effective_test_date`, `age_bracket`, `applicable_scoring_basis`, `event_modality`, `altitude`, `official_waiver_status_if_declared`, `source_edition`.
- **Evidence ledger:** raw measurement, unit, apparatus/test conditions, source/date, self-reported vs independently witnessed, technique/repetition validity, source chart and rule version, whether all events and body composition conditions were satisfied.
- **Score decisions:** validate **every required event minimum** before total/average/category. Example Army general requires both total 300 **and** each 60; Marine combat PFT 210 **within approved combat score chart**; Coast Guard all-event floor 45, then categorical rollup. Army CFT is *overall elapsed time and all completion requirements*; don't invent CFT points.
- **Three stages:** (A) pass all applicable event gates, (B) build a margin in the *weakest* domain so a normal performance fluctuation is less likely to fail, (C) pursue documented higher class/score where published, or an explicitly chosen event-level target. Different event metrics require **goal-aware** time/reps/load math; do not ask for 110% of max 100 or call “above CFT pass” a higher official CFT score.
- **Confidence label:** authoritative exact, component verified/chart missing, occupational condition unresolved, outdated/superseded, or unsupported. An offline practice log isn't military certification.
- **No guarantee:** only service or designated officials can validate military compliance, medical eligibility and official event execution; the app can coach and project *provisionally* after chart validation.
