## Rapid, evidence-aware load calibration demonstration

`synthetic_exposure()` provides a finalized exact chest-press occurrence with two actual 20 kg stack sets, 12 reps each, 2 RIR, acceptable technique and no pain, against an 8–12 / 1–3 RIR target. It deliberately includes a 99 kg prefill: the returned historical working fact is **20 kg**, never 99. This optional synthetic observation is not fabricated into the example week by default.

A complete qualifying exposure at top reps and ≥5 RIR can immediately flag possible underchallenge and expose the next supplied attainable load as **information for recalibration**, without a forced increment or waiting period. A separately finalized one-set calibration occurrence may qualify under its declared complete required-set identity; one completed set out of an ordinary two-set occurrence does not qualify. Pain, deteriorating technique, unknown effort or missing completion abstains. A substituted variant starts its own comparison cohort, with no inherited working load. Optional 1RM/e1RM remains information only. No higher inventory value means no invented increment.
