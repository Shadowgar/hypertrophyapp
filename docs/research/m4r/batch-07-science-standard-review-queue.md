# M4-R Batch 07 — Training Science Standard v1 review queue (NOT approved)

**Date:** 2026-10-16. **Release class:** research decision packet and finite approval gate, **not** an exercise prescription, executable configuration, or “world's best workout” claim. Builds on [B07 evidence closure](batch-07-quantitative-evidence-closure.md) and B04–B06. **No code until a qualified human reviewer and the owner separately accept context-bound settings and acceptance criteria.**

## I. What is already sufficiently grounded to design, not prescribe

These original product **requirements** may inform an offline human-reviewed prototype without selecting physiological numbers:

1. **Mode isolation:** Paid/source-owned *Authored* plans and user history are immutable. Opt-in *Customized* is a distinct system with independently designed movements, dates, progression and source-of-rule trace; preview does not activate.
2. **Constraint-first choice:** Choose a feasible schedule compatible with user goal, actual local calendar, available equipment, intended session cap, training recency, movement tolerance and preference. Never award “Full Body +5% gain” versus PPL from a name.
3. **Muscle-action coverage, not fabricated half sets:** Describe direct/primary, indirect/secondary and unclassified muscle-region involvement independently; no numeric equivalence weighting admitted. Track exposure at the actual performed-event level.
4. **Progression comparability:** Maintain repeatable exercise variants, ROM and loading meanings; compare reps, load, effort and outcomes from confirmed comparable sessions, leaving unknown effort unknown. A calendar tick cannot create a verified performance improvement.
5. **Fatigue honesty:** Failure is a distinct set-end mechanism, not mandatory high-quality training. Acute fatigue, recovery experience and chronic muscle-size adaptation are different concepts; pain cannot be overridden by a biological “stimulus score.”
6. **Time honesty:** Time budget includes preparation, warmups, exercise execution, actual rest, equipment transition and buffer. If only a nominal estimate fits, flag it as uncertain; if the worst-case/plausible estimate breaches a hard limit, ask the user to choose a different compromise.
7. **Consent/abstention:** Every provisional exercise and weekly exposure has rationale and uncertainty. Infeasible or insufficiently known conditions block automatic activation; no covert plan editing.

**These are product-design decisions proposed from research, not an assertion that a given program has demonstrated clinical safety or hypertrophy superiority.**

## II. Numeric evidence intake — separate observed facts from candidate values

| Decision ID | Published empirical context | Numeric idea for **expert evaluation only** | Approval status / what must be resolved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| N01 weekly work | 2026 Pelland **4 fractional** model threshold; 5–10 / 11–18 model efficiency bands; ACSM overview reports greater growth in literature with **≥10 weekly sets** | Use **6, 10 and 14 primary-action sets/week** as *contrastive simulated workloads* to test feasibility, not initial recommendations. They are deliberately distinct from Pelland's fractional unit and therefore **must not** be inferred as equivalent thresholds. | **Blocked for users**; have human reviewer choose population-, muscle-, direct/indirect- and equipment-aware starting ranges or reject all three simulation values |
| N02 work distribution | Volume-equated frequency literature often finds no independent clear hypertrophy effect; exact replications sparse | Test **2, 3, 4 and 6 opportunities per week** with constant intended volume and realistic time; include rolling split across unequal days | **Feasibility cases, not prescribed optimal days**; resolve minimum day feasibility and chosen-split exceptions |
| N03 sets/exercise/session | Multiset training often yields additional growth; incremental benefit depends on muscle, effort and total work | Synthetic slots with **1, 2 or 3 working sets** to compare time/dose, keeping author program tables out of examples | **No universal dose**; avoid inferring “one set ineffective” or “three sets always maximal” |
| N04 repetitions/load | 2017 load meta-analysis found growth with low and high loads when effort protocols aligned, strength favors heavy; studied effort/exercise definitions varied | Test flexible **6–10, 10–15 and 15–20 rep targets** as engineering examples, with other valid ranges possible. These are **not** proven optimal bands and cannot imply a specific %1RM | **Blocked default** pending movement-specific expert judgement and user skill/tolerance |
| N05 effort | Different trials compare failure with **1–3 RIR**, some compare **4–6 RIR**; small 2026 studies share one sample | Test simulated reports of **0, 2, 4 and unknown RIR**; **do not** make “2 RIR universally optimal” | **Blocked default** pending exercise-/experience-specific fatigue and RIR reliability review; no mandatory failure |
| N06 rest | 2024 nine-study meta-analysis weakly supports avoiding very short rests (~≤60 s) in some comparisons; not a fixed >90 optimum | Test assumed rest values **60, 90, 120 and 180 seconds** for scheduling sensitivity, **not** an evidence-based rest timer policy | **Blocked default** pending measured time/performance validation and session goal |
| N07 overload | 2022/2024 studies allow load or repetition progress; 2026 progressive/fixed confounded by effort and volume load | Synthetic progression comparisons: same load/more reps; same reps/slightly greater *available* load; no comparable data | **Qualitative rule candidate only**; no fixed “add 2.5 kg every week” or compulsory progression |
| N08 recovery/deload | No reliable universal deload schedule or diagnostic recovery score found | Optional review when multiple credible **comparable** sessions decline or schedule cannot be sustained, no fixed numerical trigger | **Blocked numerical trigger/duration**; human review, user consent and safety escalation |
| N09 exercise overlap | 2026 fractional-set model is *model-based* using 0, 0.5 or 1 indirect units | Show **three separate actual counts**: primary intended sets, secondary involved sets, unresolved sets; test hypothetical weighting **only in an analytical research worksheet** if later justified | **No validated automatic 0.5 weight** and no hidden exercise preference ranking |
| N10 session duration | Essentials source's ~45-minute claim can stretch to ~45–60; trial training time isn't real-gym prediction | Evaluate a **hard 45-minute limit** against preparation/execution/rest/transition/buffer; intentionally include an infeasible 60-minute candidate | **Feasibility invariant**; independent real-world measured duration calibration needed |

**Critical numerical hygiene:** Every numeric idea in column 3 is a **non-executable sensitivity/test input**; not an approved starting workout, scientific optimum, personal minimum, target percent gain, or evidence-calibrated injury/fatigue probability. Do not copy this table into code or feed these synthetic values into production. A reviewer may reject every proposed test input.

## III. Decision-ready rule proposals (human reviewer must explicitly accept/reject)

| Candidate rule | Scope allowed for review | Why it may pass qualitative review | What remains a decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prefer feasible complete plans over famous split names | All independently generated Customized planning | Split meta-analysis, self-selection, time/equipment constraints | How to disclose tradeoffs without promising equal growth |
| Identify whether a muscle has direct, indirect, no or unknown exposure | Initially major named muscle regions in healthy adults | Anatomy plus source-specific region comparisons | Human-verified ontology and confidence per action/movement |
| Allow a person to seek progression by extra comparable reps **or** feasible load | Healthy adults without identified limiting constraint | Direct trials of repetition versus load progression | Appropriateness by variant, target goal and technique |
| Avoid requiring failure on every working set | Default design philosophy | Conflicting controlled RIR outcomes, fatigue cost, safety practicality | When an informed advanced user may opt into failure |
| Carry rolling PPL, Upper/Lower and other sequences across weeks | All calendar-driven Customized plans | Mathematical schedule integrity, not biology | Handling missed days, explicit user choices and overlap |
| Decline activation if hard constraints cannot be met | All | Prevents fabricated equipment/time/date feasibility | Minimum input and preview policy |
| Preserve live user records while researching, testing or changing programs | All | Ownership/data integrity | Independent tests in isolated data only |

## IV. Finite expert/owner sign-off checklist

**Scientific reviewer** (independent qualified strength-and-conditioning professional, name/credentials/date to be recorded; none has been retained):
- [ ] Define intended healthy adult starting populations, training recency/training status, goals and explicit out-of-scope conditions.
- [ ] Define *which* muscle-action regions are counted and a defensible way to handle secondary involvement **without defaulting an unvalidated half-set factor**.
- [ ] Review and either approve, replace or reject **population-specific** initial weekly set ranges and their tradeoffs. State if initial dose differs for a returned-from-break trainee and why.
- [ ] Review proposed effort/repetition/load/rest ranges **by movement complexity, experience and equipment**, plus time feasibility, uncertainty and safe substitutions. No unsupported universal optimum claims.
- [ ] Specify what performance observations qualify load/rep progression or an intentional lighter period; document when the system must abstain.
- [ ] Independently check study overlap, trial bias/measurement limitations, human outcomes and external validity; cite unresolved high-stakes questions rather than pretending a full GRADE report exists.
- [ ] Review original scenario prototypes for physical feasibility and originality; sign acceptance or corrections.

**Owner decision**, after review:
- [ ] Approve only a versioned **bounded research-derived proposal** with scope, exceptions, evidence IDs, numerical operating ranges and explicit opt-in; or reject/ask for changes.
- [ ] Authorize a **separate implementation/design PR** only after this decision. PR #84 remains research-only and draft.
- [ ] Keep all Authored Plans, historical performed sessions, databases, and paid manuals unchanged.

## V. Exit criteria for M4-R research

**Research phase can stop expanding** once this packet and at least four original blueprint cases are documented and the quantitative unresolved items above are assigned to a named reviewer/owner decision. There is **no scientific requirement** to read another 2 GB of already hashed private manuals before conducting reviewer-led design. New systematic research should only be authorized for **a named decision that cannot be made** with the evidence already collected.

- **May proceed immediately in research:** paper blueprint review and feasibility scenarios, correction of clear source-to-evidence misreadings, proposed training ontology categories.
- **May not proceed yet:** approved numerical physiological defaults, automatic personalized program construction, claim of the “best known workout,” merging/deployment.
- **If scientific reviewer is unavailable:** remain at **qualitative design plus synthetic test inputs**, mark all numerical defaults blocked. Do **not** quietly upgrade this packet into a physician/strength-coach substitute.

This standard is a tool for **limiting false precision and finishing a bounded project**, not a demand for perpetual research or a substitute for individualized coaching.
