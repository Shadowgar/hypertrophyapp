## Settings keeps the choices independent

Theme selects Graphite, Charcoal or Classic WorkoutApp. Appearance selects Light, Dark or Follow Device Settings. The paired swatches preview both modes; the selected radio and word “Selected” provide non-color state. Each representative Settings frame uses Follow Device Settings and correctly labels its resolved device mode. See the [baseline and save/recovery sequence](SCREENS.md#59-appearance--theme-and-appearance) for the explicit Dark preference retained by existing users.
